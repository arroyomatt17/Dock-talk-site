Delete your Dock Talk account

Dock Talk LLC lets you delete your account and its data at any time. Deleting is permanent and can't be undone.

Delete it in the app

Diners: open Profile, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Restaurants: sign in, open Restaurant info, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Diner accounts: your login, profile and saved favorites.
  • Restaurant accounts: your login and profile, your restaurant listing with its hours and menus, all of your deals (including recurring deals), and the photos you uploaded.

Anonymous usage counts that aren't tied to your name or email may be kept, because they can't be linked back to you.

Can't sign in?

Email southforkdocktalk@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm and complete it within 30 days.
