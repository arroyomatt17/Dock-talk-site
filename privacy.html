Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE — 9/26/2026]

Dock Talk ("we," "us," "our") operates the Dock Talk website and app at docktalk.chat, connecting diners with restaurant specials in the South Fork of New York and, as we expand, other regions. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it. It applies to the full app — diner accounts, restaurant accounts, and the public specials feed — not just the sign-up forms.

Dock Talk is currently operated by [Matthew — sole proprietor / Dock Talk LLC, a New York limited liability company — update once formed].

What we collect

If you create a diner account: your name, email address, town, and a password (stored securely — we never see or store your password in readable form; it is hashed by our authentication provider). If you favorite restaurants, we store which restaurants you've favorited against your account.

If you create a restaurant account: your name, restaurant name, town, phone number, email address, and password, plus anything you choose to add to your restaurant profile — hours, address, menu text, photos you upload, and the specials you post (text, time slot, expiration, and an optional photo).

We do not use tracking pixels or third-party advertising networks, and we do not run analytics beyond basic hosting logs kept by our hosting provider.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign back in
  • To show diners the specials restaurants in their area have posted, and to let diners save favorite restaurants
  • To let restaurants manage their profile, menu, and daily specials
  • To review and approve new restaurant accounts before they can post publicly
  • To contact you about your account or about Dock Talk itself

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Account data, specials, and uploaded photos are stored with Supabase, our database, authentication, and file-storage provider. The website and app are hosted by Netlify. Both providers act as data processors on our behalf under their own security and privacy practices — we don't operate our own servers. Some of our early outreach and interest-list forms, separate from the app itself, may still run through Formspree, a third-party form service.

Dock Talk operates across more than one country (currently the United States, with other regions planned). Your information may be stored and processed in a country other than the one you're in.

Restaurant-posted content

Menus, specials, and photos posted by restaurants are visible to the public on the diner-facing feed. Restaurants are responsible for the accuracy of what they post — see our Terms of Use for details.

Your choices

  • You can update or remove information from your restaurant profile at any time from your dashboard.
  • You can un-favorite a restaurant at any time from the diner feed.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and associated data, or to export a copy of what we hold about you, by emailing us below.
  • You can ask to be removed from any early-access or interest list at any time.

Children's privacy

Dock Talk is not directed at children, and we ask that only people 18 or older create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

As Dock Talk grows — new regions, new features, new service providers — we'll update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. If a change is significant, we'll make a reasonable effort to let account holders know directly.

Contact
